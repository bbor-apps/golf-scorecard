Holes 1–6 Scramble

Both players tee off on every hole. The team picks the best drive, then both hit from that spot. Keep choosing the best shot and hitting from there until the ball is holed.

→Both players tee off
→Choose best ball
→Both hit from that spot
→Repeat until holed
→One team score per hole
Holes 7–12 Partner Alternate

Each player always has their own ball in play — but your partner hits your next shot. You alternate hitting each other's ball until both are holed. Team score is the better of the two balls.

Example — Ty & Baker
Ty's Ball Ty tees off → Baker hits → Ty hits → Baker hits…
Baker's Ball Baker tees off → Ty hits → Baker hits → Ty hits…
→Both players always have their own ball
→Your partner always hits your next shot
→Team score = better of the two balls
Holes 13–18 Chapman

Both players tee off, then hit each other's drive. After those second shots, you choose the best ball and play alternate shot from there until holed. One team score per hole.

1Both players tee off
2Both hit partner's drive
3Choose best second shot
4Alternate shot from there until holed
Scoring Match Play

Each hole is its own battle. Win the hole, go 1 up. Tie the hole, it's halved. The segment (6 holes) is won when a team can't be caught — if you're 2 up with 1 to play, the segment is yours. 1 point per segment, 3 points available per match per day.

→1 point per 6-hole segment
→3 segments = 3 points per match
→Segment won when lead > holes remaining
→Max double bogey pickup
→Inside 3 feet = good
